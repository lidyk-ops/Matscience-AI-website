What the models don't do

They cover single-phase liquids and homogeneous mixtures. They don't cover nanofluids, suspensions or undisclosed inhibitor packages, and they don't replace corrosion, compatibility or service life testing. When a molecule falls outside what the models know, they say so.

Develop a new material with us

If you have an application and no fluid that meets it, we'll work on it together. Ownership and confidentiality are agreed before any data is shared.

  1. You bring the application

    The performance target, the operating conditions, and what the material has to survive.

  2. We narrow the candidates

    Our models screen the options and propose the formulations worth making.

  3. We make and test together

    Candidates go to the lab, using standard ASTM methods where they apply.

  4. Results sharpen the model

    Each measurement feeds the next round, until a formulation is ready to scale.
