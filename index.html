Liquid coolants for direct-to-chip cooling

Three fluids for cold plate loops in AI data centers. Tell us about your loop and we'll send the datasheet, safety data sheet and sample details for the one that fits.

What does your loop need?
Pick the option closest to your loop and we'll point you to the right coolant.

MS-PG25

Available

A 25% propylene glycol coolant with a phosphate-free organic acid inhibitor package. For loops designed around PG25, the fluid the Open Compute Project's guidelines are written for.

Glycol
25% propylene glycol
Inhibitors
Phosphate-free organic acid
Use
Direct-to-chip cold plate loops
Request MS-PG25 datasheet

MS-DC6

Available

A water-based, low-glycol coolant with a corrosion inhibitor package, formulated and made in-house. For loops where you want less glycol in the fluid than a PG25 carries.

Glycol
Low glycol, water-based
Inhibitors
Corrosion inhibitor package, made in-house
Use
Direct-to-chip cold plate loops
Request MS-DC6 datasheet
