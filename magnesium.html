Magnesium alloys

Magnesium is the lightest structural metal. We're applying the approach behind our coolants to magnesium alloys for lightweight parts: models first, then the lab, then production.

Register interest See our coolants

Why magnesium

At 1.74 g/cm³, magnesium weighs about two-thirds as much as aluminium and under a quarter as much as steel. That makes it a strong candidate wherever weight matters: vehicle parts, electronics housings and aerospace components.

What we're working on

Two properties decide where magnesium can be used. They're where our development effort goes.

Corrosion resistance

Magnesium corrodes more readily than aluminium or steel, which limits where it can go. We're developing alloys aimed at closing that gap.

Formability

Magnesium's hexagonal crystal structure makes it harder to shape at room temperature than aluminium. Our models help screen compositions and processing routes before they reach the lab.
