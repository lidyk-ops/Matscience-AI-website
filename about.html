How we turn research into products

Every material we make goes through the same three stages.

  1. Model

    Machine learning trained on more than a million published property measurements, combined with physics-based engineering models, screens candidates before anything reaches the bench.

  2. Make and test

    The most promising formulations go to the lab, tested with standard ASTM methods where they apply. Each result feeds the next round of modelling.

  3. Produce

    30+ years of manufacturing experience on the team takes a formula to production, and then into running AI data centers.
